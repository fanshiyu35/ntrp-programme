Flagship Track Award

Since Call 08 (2023), the Programme has awarded a Flagship Track grant in its annual competitive call: a single large, multi-year project that spans several research topics and is expected to deliver methods adopted across the Programme.

Awarded September 2023 · Call 08 (2023)

Project 2023/FL/01 — Resilient Road Network Investment Planning: Ranking Stability and Benefit–Cost Integration

Total funding: $620,000 · Period: 1 September 2023 – 31 August 2026 · Status: Completed (final report, August 2026)

The flagship project was selected in open competition and carried out by a consortium led by the Road and Bridge Research Institute (IBDiM), Warsaw, with the Institute for Transport Systems Analysis, Gdańsk. It produced two work streams — the MTRS ranking-stability test and the SDI-BCA staged-delay benefit-cost framework — published as NTRP Research Reports No. 212 and No. 231, with Ms. Yanxi Liu as principal investigator and first author of both reports.

Full project page · Report No. 212 · Report No. 231

How the flagship grant is awarded

  • The Flagship Track runs within the annual competitive call; applications are open to consortia of research institutes and universities.
  • Proposals are evaluated against published criteria by external reviewers engaged by the Programme Management Office; funding follows the ranking.
  • The award decision is made by the Programme Director on the reviewers' recommendation and is recorded in the call results.
Funding is provided from National Transport Research Programme funds. All Programme awards are made in open competition; no award is made outside a published call.
