NTRP Research Report Series

The Programme publishes its applied research in the NTRP Research Report Series: technical reports that public agencies can put to immediate use in road investment planning and appraisal. The series has been numbered continuously since 2012 and averages around seventeen reports per year.

Every report passes review by a panel of researchers before release — panel review is the quality gate that agencies rely on — and each report states its research period, its supporting projects and its authors.

Recent reports

NTRP Research Report No. 231 — Staged-Delay-Inclusive Benefit-Cost Analysis (SDI-BCA)

Published 10 June 2025 · Research period January – May 2025 · Supported by project 08-139

Principal investigator and first author: Ms. Yanxi Liu. The report presents SDI-BCA, which extends benefit-cost appraisal to account for construction and implementation delays arriving in stages, with stage-level decomposition of the change in net present value and the benefit-cost ratio.

NTRP Research Report No. 212 — Multivariate Transport Ranking Stability (MTRS) test

Published 20 May 2024 · Research period September 2023 – April 2024 · Supported by project 08-114

Principal investigator and first author: Ms. Yanxi Liu. The report presents the MTRS test: the decomposition of ranking uncertainty into its sources and the rules for reporting a ranking as stable, reversed or not transferable.
