NTRP Research Report No. 212

Multivariate Transport Ranking Stability (MTRS) test

Report numberNTRP Research Report No. 212 (NTRP Research Report Series)
Published20 May 2024
Research periodSeptember 2023 – April 2024
Supporting projectProject 08-114 (Call 08, 2023)
Principal investigator / first authorMs. Yanxi Liu
Co-authorsProf. dr hab. inż. Mieczysław Osial · Elif Akarca · Daniel Onslow
ReviewReviewed by the project review panel in three meetings (February – April 2024); approved on 29 April 2024

Summary

The report presents the Multivariate Transport Ranking Stability (MTRS) test — a procedure that reports, for any set of ranked transport scenarios or projects, how firmly the ranking holds across the recognized sources of uncertainty: model structure, parameters, data vintage, policy mapping, weights, and metropolitan area structure. The test decomposes ranking uncertainty into these sources, tests whether observed changes in rank are significant, and requires explicit reporting of every reversal. Every analysis ends in a verdict a reviewer can check: stable, reversed, or not transferable.

Adoption

The MTRS test subsequently became the basis of Standard Practice SP-41, Reporting on the Stability of Scenario Rankings, published on 30 September 2024, and of Technical Guidance Note TGN-2025-01 (30 March 2025). A reference implementation of the test is maintained as open-source software.
