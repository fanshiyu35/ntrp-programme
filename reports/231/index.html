NTRP Research Report No. 231

Staged-Delay-Inclusive Benefit-Cost Analysis (SDI-BCA)

Report numberNTRP Research Report No. 231 (NTRP Research Report Series)
Published10 June 2025
Research periodJanuary – May 2025
Supporting projectProject 08-139 (Call 08, 2023)
Principal investigator / first authorMs. Yanxi Liu
Co-authorsProf. dr hab. inż. Mieczysław Osial · Kasra Amiri
ReviewReviewed by the project review panel in three meetings (March – May 2025); approved on 28 May 2025

Summary

The report presents Staged-Delay-Inclusive Benefit-Cost Analysis (SDI-BCA), which extends conventional benefit-cost analysis by modelling implementation stages and the delays that propagate between them, so that a delayed stage's effect on downstream stages, on net present value and on the benefit-cost ratio is reported explicitly. The analysis yields a stage-level marginal decomposition of the change in net present value and the benefit-cost ratio, with interaction terms reported separately.

Adoption

SDI-BCA is operationalised for practitioners in Technical Guidance Note TGN-2025-02 (15 October 2025), and is applied alongside the ranking-stability diagnostics of Report No. 212 in Technical Guidance Note TGN-2026-01 (8 May 2026).
