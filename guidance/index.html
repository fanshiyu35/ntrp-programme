Technical Guidance Notes (TGN)

Adopted methods are operationalised for practitioners in Technical Guidance Notes (TGN), issued by the Programme's Technical Committee. Each note states the method it implements, the underlying research reports and the standard practice it conforms to.

30 March 2025

TGN-2025-01 — Guidance on the Application of MTRS in Road Network Ranking Studies

Application guidance for the Multivariate Transport Ranking Stability (MTRS) test of NTRP Research Report No. 212, following the publication of Standard Practice SP-41 (30 September 2024). Covers the reporting of stability verdicts (stable, reversed, not transferable) and the variance decomposition behind them.

15 October 2025

TGN-2025-02 — Guidance on Staged-Delay-Inclusive Benefit-Cost Analysis for Road Projects

Application guidance for Staged-Delay-Inclusive Benefit-Cost Analysis (SDI-BCA) of NTRP Research Report No. 231. Covers stage dependency graphs, propagation of delays with fractional-year pro-rating, and the stage-level marginal decomposition of changes in net present value and the benefit-cost ratio.

8 May 2026

TGN-2026-01 — Integrated Guidance on Resilience Assessment and Benefit-Cost Analysis for the National Road Network

Integrated guidance applying ranking-stability reporting and staged-delay benefit-cost appraisal together in national road network studies, incorporating the findings of independent replication work published in 2025–2026.

Copies of the guidance notes are provided to institutions adopting the methods and are available from the Programme Management Office on request. Each note carries the Programme reference number, a version page and the Technical Committee roster.
