About the Programme

The National Transport Research Programme (NTRP; Narodowy Program Badań Transportowych, NPBT) is the joint national research programme of the Polish Transport Research Institutes Network (POLTRIN). It was established in January 2021 for the programme period 2021–2030 and is managed from its Programme Management Office in Warsaw.

The Programme commissions research through annual competitive calls, publishes its applied results in the NTRP Research Report Series (numbered continuously since 2012) and in Technical Guidance Notes, and maintains standing topic review panels. Programme overview · Calls · Reports.
