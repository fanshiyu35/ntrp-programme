Competitive Calls

Research under the Programme is commissioned through annual competitive calls. Each call batch comprises a topics track — proposals for new research topics, evaluated by the standing topic review panels — and a projects track — full project proposals, evaluated by external reviewers engaged by the Programme Management Office.

Flagship Track

Since Call 08 (2023) the Programme has run a Flagship Track for a small number of large, multi-year projects that span several topics. Projects in this track are selected in open competition and are expected to deliver methods adopted across the Programme. The first flagship award went to project 2023/FL/01 — Resilient Road Network Investment Planning: Ranking Stability and Benefit–Cost Integration ($620,000, 1 September 2023 – 31 August 2026).

How proposals are evaluated

  • Topic proposals are evaluated by a standing topic review panel under terms of reference approved by the Programme Director. Panels recommend topics for funding and examine the completion report drafts of funded topics.
  • Project proposals are evaluated by external reviewers engaged on the basis of published research record; reviews are conducted remotely and in writing.
  • Evaluation criteria are published with each call announcement; all decisions are recommendations to the Programme Director, who awards funding.
Call documents, evaluation criteria and result notices are distributed through the Programme Office. Funded work is published on the projects and topics pages.
