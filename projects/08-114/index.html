Multivariate ranking stability methods for regional transport network planning

About the project

This project incubated the Multivariate Transport Ranking Stability (MTRS) test: the decomposition of ranking uncertainty into its sources, the rules for reporting a ranking as stable, reversed or not transferable, and the accompanying reference implementation. The principal output is NTRP Research Report No. 212, published on 20 May 2024, with Ms. Yanxi Liu as principal investigator and first author. The MTRS test subsequently became the basis of Standard Practice SP-41, "Reporting on the Stability of Scenario Rankings," published on 30 September 2024.
