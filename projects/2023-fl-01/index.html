Resilient Road Network Investment Planning: Ranking Stability and Benefit–Cost Integration

About the project

The project develops methods for resilient road network investment planning: a multivariate test of the stability of scenario rankings, and a benefit-cost framework that accounts for construction and implementation delays arriving in stages. The two work streams were pursued as the programme projects 08-114 and 08-139.

Outputs

  • NTRP Research Report No. 212 — Multivariate Transport Ranking Stability (MTRS) test. Published 20 May 2024. Principal investigator and first author: Ms. Yanxi Liu.
  • NTRP Research Report No. 231 — Staged-Delay-Inclusive Benefit-Cost Analysis (SDI-BCA). Published 10 June 2025. Principal investigator and first author: Ms. Yanxi Liu.
  • Support to Standard Practice SP-41, "Reporting on the Stability of Scenario Rankings," published 30 September 2024.
  • Technical Guidance Notes: TGN-2025-01 (30 March 2025); TGN-2025-02 (15 October 2025); TGN-2026-01 (8 May 2026).
  • Adoption wave: the MTRS test and SDI-BCA have been incorporated in the internal practice of institutions in Poland and abroad, 2025–2026.

Funding

The project was funded from National Transport Research Programme funds, awarded in the programme's annual competitive call (Call 08, 2023).
