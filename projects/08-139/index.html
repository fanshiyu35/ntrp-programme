PROJECT 08-139

Implementation support for staged-delay inclusive benefit-cost analysis in road investment appraisal

StatusCompleted
ProgrammeNTRP Call 08 (2023) — selected in open competition from seven eligible proposals
Period1 September 2024 – 31 August 2026
Total funding$380,000 (National Transport Research Programme funds)

Project lead

  • Dr. Tamsin Argyle — senior researcher

Project members

  • Ms. Yanxi Liu — researcher (remote participation)
  • Additional researchers of the consortium institutions

About the project

This project supports the implementation of Standard Practice SP-41 in road investment appraisal and develops the Staged-Delay-Inclusive Benefit-Cost Analysis (SDI-BCA), which extends benefit-cost appraisal to account for construction and implementation delays arriving in stages. The principal output is NTRP Research Report No. 231, published on 10 June 2025, with Ms. Yanxi Liu as principal investigator and first author. The method is operationalised for practitioners in Technical Guidance Note TGN-2025-02 (15 October 2025).
