Projects & Topics

All projects and topics listed below were selected in open competition under the Programme's annual calls. Each page records the call batch, the review panel or evaluation route, the funding awarded and the team.

Flagship projects

Flagship Track · Call 08 (2023)

Project 2023/FL/01 — Resilient Road Network Investment Planning: Ranking Stability and Benefit–Cost Integration

$620,000 · 1 September 2023 – 31 August 2026 · Status: Completed (final report, August 2026)

Developed the MTRS ranking-stability test and the SDI-BCA staged-delay benefit-cost framework; outputs published as NTRP Research Reports No. 212 and No. 231 and operationalised in Technical Guidance Notes TGN-2025-01, TGN-2025-02 and TGN-2026-01.

Projects funded under Call 08 (2023)

Call 08 (2023)

Project 08-114 — Multivariate ranking stability methods for regional transport network planning

$450,000 · 1 January 2024 – 31 May 2025 · Selected in open competition from nine eligible proposals · Status: Completed

Incubated the Multivariate Transport Ranking Stability (MTRS) test; principal output NTRP Research Report No. 212 (20 May 2024).

Call 08 (2023)

Project 08-139 — Implementation support for staged-delay inclusive benefit-cost analysis in road investment appraisal

$380,000 · 1 September 2024 – 31 August 2026 · Selected in open competition from seven eligible proposals · Status: Completed

Developed the Staged-Delay-Inclusive Benefit-Cost Analysis (SDI-BCA); principal output NTRP Research Report No. 231 (10 June 2025).
