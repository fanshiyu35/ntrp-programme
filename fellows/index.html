Research Fellows

The Programme appoints research fellows to its projects, review panels and methods work. Fellows participate from their home institutions, and remote participation is a standing feature of the Programme's working model: evaluations are submitted in writing and panel sessions are held by video conference.

Fellow appointments

Fellow appointments are made by the Programme Management Office on the strength of published research record. A fellow's appointment, projects and review service are recorded in the Programme's personnel and review records, and are confirmed in writing by the Programme Office when requested by the fellow's institution or by a funding or examining body.
