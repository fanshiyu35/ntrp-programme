The National Transport Research Programme (NTRP; Narodowy Program Badań Transportowych, NPBT) produces transport planning and appraisal methods that public agencies can put to immediate use in road investment planning. It is the first joint national research programme of the Polish Transport Research Institutes Network (POLTRIN, formed in 2018), established in January 2021 for the programme period 2021–2030 and managed from its Programme Management Office in Warsaw.

What the Programme does

  • Annual competitive calls. Research topics and projects are funded through open competition. Call batches are numbered from 01 (2021); proposals are evaluated by standing review panels and by external reviewers.
  • Research reports. Applied results are published in the NTRP Research Report Series, numbered continuously since 2012. Every report passes review by a panel of researchers before release.
  • Technical guidance. Adopted methods are operationalised for practitioners in Technical Guidance Notes (TGN), issued by the Programme's Technical Committee.
  • Topic review panels. Standing panels evaluate topic proposals in each call batch and examine the completion report drafts of funded topics (review panel pages).

Flagship and funded work

Flagship Project 2023/FL/01 — Resilient Road Network Investment Planning

Awarded in the 2023 competitive call (Call 08, Flagship Track) · $620,000 · 1 September 2023 – 31 August 2026 · Completed

The programme's flagship project developed a multivariate test of the stability of scenario rankings (the MTRS test) and a benefit-cost framework for delays arriving in stages (SDI-BCA). Both methods were published in the NTRP Research Report Series and have since been adopted in standard practice and in national guidance.
