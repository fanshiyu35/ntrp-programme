Topic Review Panels

The National Transport Research Programme (NTRP) maintains standing topic review panels for the annual competitive calls. Each panel evaluates the topic proposals submitted in a call batch and examines the completion report drafts of funded topics. Panels work to written terms of reference approved by the Programme Director.

Mandate

Each panel, within its term:

  1. evaluates topic proposals submitted under the corresponding call batch (Batch 11 for Panel A, Batch 12 for Panel B, Batch 13 for Panel C) and recommends topics for funding;
  2. examines completion report drafts of funded topics and advises the Programme Office on acceptance;
  3. reports its recommendations to the Programme Director.

Members are drawn from researchers whose published work has shaped the methods applied across the Programme. Panel members serve in a personal capacity, participate remotely, and are expected to serve for the panel term only. Written evaluations are submitted through the Programme's review portal; panel sessions are held by video conference. An honorarium is payable in accordance with the Programme's standard reviewer terms.

2025/2026 programme year in figures

Across the three panels of the 2025/2026 programme year: 14 topic proposals were evaluated (5 under Call Batch 11, 5 under Call Batch 12, 4 under Call Batch 13) and 6 completion report drafts were examined.
