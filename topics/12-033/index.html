Topic 12-033 — Resilience Screening of the National Road Network under Climate Stress

(Polish title: Badanie odporności krajowej sieci drogowej na ekstremalne warunki klimatyczne)

Topic number12-033
Call batchBatch 12 (competitive call, 2026)
Proposal reviewTopic Review Panel B, January – April 2026
Budget$260,000
Period1 July 2026 – 30 June 2028
Lead institutionŁysa Góra Transport Research Institute, Kielce, Poland
Principal investigatorDr. Bożena Ligocka
StatusIn progress

Summary

The topic develops screening procedures for identifying road sections whose flood, heat and freeze-thaw exposure would, under credible climate stress scenarios, cause disproportionate network disruption. Scenario rankings are reported with stability diagnostics, following the reporting practice established in Standard Practice SP-41 (2024), which draws on the MTRS test of NTRP Research Report No. 212.

Outputs

Interim report due June 2027; final topic report in the NTRP Research Report Series.
