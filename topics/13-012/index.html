Topic 13-012 — Multimodal Freight Demand Forecasting along V4 Corridors

(Polish title: Prognozowanie popytu w przewozach intermodalnych w korytarzach V4)

Topic number13-012
Call batchBatch 13 (competitive call, 2026)
Proposal reviewTopic Review Panel C, June – September 2026
Budget$280,000
Period1 January 2027 – 31 December 2028
Lead institutionVistula Freight Research Centre, Warsaw, Poland
Principal investigatorDr. Sana Bhattacharya
StatusApproved (start 1 January 2027)

Summary

The topic develops corridor-level freight demand forecasts for the V4 rail-road corridors and evaluates the sensitivity of corridor investment rankings to forecast uncertainty and to implementation delays, applying the staged-delay treatment of NTRP Research Report No. 231 (Staged-Delay-Inclusive Benefit-Cost Analysis, 2025) alongside the ranking-stability diagnostics of NTRP Research Report No. 212.

Outputs

Interim report due June 2028; final topic report in the NTRP Research Report Series.
