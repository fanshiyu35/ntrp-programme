Topic 11-021 — Data-Driven Network Safety Screening for Regional Road Corridors

(Polish title: Identyfikacja zagrożeń bezpieczeństwa sieci drogowej w oparciu o analizy danych (korytarze regionalne))

Topic number11-021
Call batchBatch 11 (competitive call, 2025)
Proposal reviewTopic Review Panel A, September – December 2025
Budget$240,000
Period1 January 2026 – 30 June 2027
Lead institutionBaltic Institute for Network Analytics, Gdańsk, Poland
Principal investigatorDr. Witold Skibiński
StatusIn progress

Summary

The topic develops screening procedures that combine road safety records, traffic counts and road-condition data to identify regional corridors where safety interventions would yield the largest expected reduction in casualties. The screening framework builds on the Programme's published work on ranking stability, including the Multivariate Transport Ranking Stability (MTRS) test of NTRP Research Report No. 212, and reports the stability of the resulting priority rankings.

Outputs

Interim report due December 2026; final topic report in the NTRP Research Report Series.
